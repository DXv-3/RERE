## Slice 2 — Jev Advisory With Measured Cost

With Ryan's $0.10/day selection cap, first implement durable admission and reconciliation; then one bounded external judgment over a redacted fixture, validated response, exact routing charge, and deterministic fallback. Show task requirement and policy reasons without inventing model reasoning. Compare with manual/deterministic baselines; expand to at least 50 representative cases before automatic routing. Evidence: acceptance results, cost/latency report, and no provider changes caused by advisory mode.

## Slice 3 — Automatic Selection Within A Verified Harness

Enable explicit opt-in only for qualified models/accounts and bounded execution adapters. Make route reservation, durable receipt, and effect replay safe. Pin the selected account/model during the turn. Include parallel-client and crash-recovery tests. Evidence: completed task, receipt, cost reconciliation and one-click return to manual. Unsupported metered harnesses remain manual.

## Slice 4 — Cross-Provider Continuity

Reuse existing handoff service at completed-turn boundaries. Exercise original-goal retention, artifact/checkpoint references, context limits, unresolved approvals and uncertain tool outcomes. No mid-tool switch. Evidence: a two-provider task with retained requirements and no repeated side effects; clear block when coverage cannot fit.
